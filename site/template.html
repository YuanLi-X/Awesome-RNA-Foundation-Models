      How the gel stays current

      A GitHub Action searches arXiv, bioRxiv and Crossref every day. New candidates are classified against the scope rules and opened as a pull request, and a maintainer reviews each one before it is merged.

      What counts as a model

      A reusable backbone or checkpoint whose pre-training corpus explicitly includes RNA-derived sequences, and that supports embeddings, fine-tuning or generation. Downstream-only predictors, 3D structure systems and single-cell models are out of scope.
